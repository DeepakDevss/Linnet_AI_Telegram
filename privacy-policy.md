# Privacy Policy — Linnet AI Telegram Bot

*Last updated: 16 October 2026*

## 1. Who We Are

Linnet AI ("we", "us") runs the Telegram bot @Linnet_Agents_Bot. If you need to get in touch, write to coresovereign.official@gmail.com.

## 2. What We Collect

When you use the bot, we may collect:

- Your Telegram user ID, username, and first name
- Chat ID, chat type, chat title, and language
- The messages you send to the bot — text, voice, and images
- Usage details such as message count, when you were first and last seen, and the topics you tend to discuss

## 3. How We Use Your Data

- To reply to your messages and provide AI assistance
- To keep conversation memory, so the bot can follow the thread
- To spot abuse and stop misuse
- To improve the bot, our apps, and our AI — using aggregated, anonymized statistics only

## 4. Legal Basis (GDPR)

- Consent (Art. 6(1)(a)) — this applies when you message the bot
- Legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f)) — for abuse prevention and improving the service

## 5. Third-Party AI Providers

We may pass your messages to:

- Google Gemini — text, vision, and text-to-speech
- Groq — text and Whisper speech-to-text

These providers handle your data under their own privacy policies. We never sell your data. Your data may be transferred to and processed in countries outside your own — including the United States, where Google and Groq operate. Where the law requires it, we rely on standard contractual clauses.

## 6. Data Retention (Purpose-Bound)

We keep data only for as long as each purpose needs it:

- Conversation memory: 90 days from your last interaction, then purged automatically
- User profile (pseudonymous identifiers, name, language): 18 months from your last interaction, then deleted
- Server and error logs: 30 days at most; user identifiers in logs are hashed with a rotating salt
- Aggregated, non-identifiable statistics (topic counts with no user linkage, k-anonymity suppression, differential privacy noise): kept indefinitely to improve the service

We enforce these limits with automated purge jobs.

## 7. How We Improve the Bot

Improvement relies only on aggregated, anonymized statistics. We don't retrain models on your raw conversations, and we don't sell your data.

## 8. Your Rights

You can:

- Use /forget in the bot to delete your stored data
- Email coresovereign.official@gmail.com to ask for access, correction, or deletion
- Lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority

## 9. Children

The bot isn't meant for anyone under 13, or under 16 if you're in the European Union. We don't knowingly collect data from children.

## 10. Security

We use reasonable technical measures to protect your data. No system is fully secure, so please use the bot at your own discretion.

## 11. Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. If you keep using the bot after a change, that counts as acceptance.
